Our Story

Good Cars. Honest People. No Nonsense.

At Heritage & Horsepower, we believe finding a car should feel exciting, not complicated.

Our collection brings together cars with character, style, and something special about them. Whether you have a particular model in mind or simply enjoy discovering great cars, we're here to help you find the right fit.

Take a closer look, ask a few questions, and take your time. The right car doesn't need a hard sell.

  • Cars with Character

    A carefully presented collection of cars, each with its own style and personality.

  • A Straightforward Experience

    Clear information, helpful answers, and the freedom to find the car that's right for you.

  • Your Next Great Drive

    From your first browse to choosing your next car, we're here to help make the journey easier.

How It Works

Three Steps to Your Keys

  1. Browse the collection

    Look through the cars on this page, filter by make and fuel type, and open any car for the full details.

  2. Visit and test drive

    Send us a message, pick a time, and come and see the car in person. Tea is on us.

  3. Drive it home

    We sort out the paperwork with you, hand over the keys, and your car is ready for the road.
